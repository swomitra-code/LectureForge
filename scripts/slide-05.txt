If we put all renewables into one bucket, we miss the most interesting part of the story.

Remember the prediction you made a moment ago. Which renewable sources seem to be changing most dramatically?

<break time="1.0s" />

Biomass has been important for a long time, and hydropower is relatively mature. But wind and solar show much more dramatic growth in the later years.

So renewable energy is simultaneously an old part of the U.S. energy system and a rapidly changing one. The total matters, but the changing mix inside the category tells us much more about the transition.
